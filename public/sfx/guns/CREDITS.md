# Gunshot sounds

Recordings from **The Free Firearm Sound Library** by Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras and
Matthew Nanney, released under **CC0** (no rights reserved; credit not required, but given with thanks):
https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library

Trimmed to the shot and its echo, kept in stereo and resampled to 48 kHz (16-bit):

| Clip | Source file | Firearm |
|---|---|---|
| pistol.wav | 1911/A_42P.wav | Colt 1911, .45 pistol |
| revolver.wav | Smith & Wesson 642/V_27P.wav | Smith & Wesson 642, .38 Special revolver |
| shotgun.wav | Nova/O_21P.wav | Benelli Nova, 12 gauge pump-action shotgun |
| sniper.wav | Tikka/W_29P.wav | Tikka T3, .30-06 bolt-action rifle |
| smg.wav | PPSh/P_32P.wav | PPSh-41 submachine gun, short burst |
